01 / What we dispatch

One dispatch partner for three very different trailers.

Dry van, reefer, and flatbed do not share the same risks. We build a separate checklist for each, so the details that cost you money get confirmed before you roll.

Dry van / 53'
01 / DRY VAN

General freight, booked without the guesswork.

Dry van is the volume side of the business. We source palletized and floor-loaded freight from vetted brokers, confirm accessorials before you commit, and prioritize lanes that get you home or into your next backhaul.

General freight Drop and hook Backhauls
  • Retail, CPG, and building supply freight
  • Broker authority and credit verified first
  • Appointment windows and check calls managed
Reefer / temperature controlled
02 / REEFER

Temperature, appointment, and rate confirmed first.

Reefer freight punishes assumptions. We verify the temperature setting, pre-cool requirements, and appointment before a rate confirmation reaches you, then keep the broker informed when weather or dock delays change the timeline.

Produce and protein Frozen and chilled Pre-cool verified
  • Temperature and trailer spec confirmed in writing
  • Lumper, detention, and reconsignment terms checked
  • Receiver requirements reviewed up front
Flatbed / 48' and 53'
03 / FLATBED

Securement, tarping, and dims handled before booking.

Flatbed freight moves on details. We confirm load dimensions, weight distribution, tarp requirements, and forklift or crane access with the broker, so you are not surprised at the shipper's gate.

Steel and lumber Machinery Tarped loads
  • Dimensions, weight, and overhang confirmed
  • Tarping and securement requirements in writing
  • Shipper access and loading equipment checked

Power only is reviewed case by case when it fits the lanes we already work. Ask about it on your call.

02 / Dispatch support

The work between the load and the delivery.

Booking the load is only the first half. The second half is everything that keeps the day from falling apart after you leave the shipper.

Load sourcing and lane planning

We work load boards, direct shippers, and broker relationships against your lane plan. You get loads that fit the truck, not a list to sort through.

Rate negotiation

We counter with market context, not guesswork. Fuel, detention, accessorials, and deadhead all factor into the number we recommend.

Broker communication

We handle the calls, emails, and portal updates. You get the rate confirmation and appointment details, not a chain of forwarded messages.

Appointment and route coordination

Pickup windows, delivery cutoffs, and transit time are planned together. When a receiver changes the schedule, we rework the day with you.

Paperwork and admin support

Rate confirmations, BOLs, lumper receipts, and PODs. We keep the file clean so invoicing does not stall two weeks after delivery.

Carrier setup and broker packets

We keep your authority, insurance, and W-9 ready for broker onboarding so a good load does not wait on missing paperwork.

08 / Carrier requirements

What it takes to get on the board.

We keep the bar clear because brokers and shippers hold us to it. Every requirement below protects your rate and your delivery record as much as ours.

01

Active MC and DOT authority

You operate under your own authority. We verify status before any load is offered.

02

Active insurance

Auto liability and cargo coverage on file, with the certificate naming the correct insured entity.

03

One year of authority preferred

Aged MC authority helps with broker setup. Newer authorities are reviewed case by case.

04

Equipment that fits the freight

53' dry van, 53' reefer, or 48'/53' flatbed. Trailers must be road-ready and correctly spec'd.

05

Clean safety profile

DOT compliance, current inspections, and a safety record that can pass a broker's vetting.

06

Completed W-9

Required for broker setup and settlement. Use the legal name tied to your authority.

07

Signed dispatch agreement

A short written agreement that sets expectations for both sides. Reviewed together on your call.

08

Direct communication

A phone number that reaches you or your driver during the day, and honest updates on availability.
