Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how carrier documents are handled.

Questions Email 3tdispatchservices@gmail.com or call 404-429-0224.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

3T Dispatch Services ("3T," "we," "us") operates www.3tdispatchservices.com. This policy explains what information we collect through the website, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

We collect the information you enter into our forms, including your name, company name, phone number, email address, equipment type, MC and DOT numbers, insurance details, fleet size, home base, lane preferences, and any notes you choose to share. Carrier onboarding also allows you to upload documents such as your MC authority letter, certificate of insurance, and W-9, and to provide an electronic signature.

We use this information to respond to dispatch requests, verify carrier eligibility, prepare broker setup packets, communicate about loads, and maintain required business records.

Documents and signatures

Carrier documents are used for verification and broker onboarding. Access is limited to personnel who need them for dispatch, compliance, or accounting. Electronic signatures are stored with the application as a record of authorization.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider may collect standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring pages, and request timestamps for security and performance purposes. We do not currently run advertising trackers or sell personal information.

Some pages embed Google Maps and load fonts from Google Fonts. Those third-party services may receive technical data such as your IP address when the page loads, subject to their own privacy policies.

How we share information

We share information with brokers and shippers only as needed to set up your carrier profile and book freight under your authority. We may also share information with service providers who support our website, email, or accounting, or when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

We retain carrier applications, documents, and dispatch records for as long as needed to operate the dispatch relationship and to meet legal, tax, and insurance requirements. You may ask us to delete information that we are not required to keep.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you submitted by emailing 3tdispatchservices@gmail.com. You can also ask us to stop contacting you about dispatch services.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is intended for commercial carriers and shippers. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services change. The updated date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
